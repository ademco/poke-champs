# Status conditions in Champions vs Scarlet/Violet

## Paralysis
A paralyzed Pokémon has its Speed halved, as in the main series. The chance to be fully paralyzed (unable to move) on a turn is 1/8 (12.5%) in Champions, compared with 1/4 (25%) in Scarlet and Violet.

## Sleep
Sleep is shorter in Champions. A sleeping Pokémon misses one or two turns: there is a one-in-three chance it wakes on its second turn, otherwise it wakes on its third turn. In Scarlet and Violet sleep lasts one to three turns.

## Freeze
A frozen Pokémon has a 25% chance to thaw each turn and is guaranteed to thaw on its third turn, so it misses at most two turns. In Scarlet and Violet the thaw chance is 20% per turn with no guaranteed thaw.

## Burn and poison
Champions does not override burn or poison in Showdown's implementation, so they work as in the main series. A burned Pokémon's physical attacks deal half damage (unless it has Guts or uses Facade).
