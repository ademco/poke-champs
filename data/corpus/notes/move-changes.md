# Moves that changed in Champions

## Power and accuracy changes
Compared with Scarlet and Violet, these legal moves have different power or accuracy in Champions:
Apple Acid 80 to 90 power; Beak Blast 100 to 120; Bone Rush 25 to 30; Fire Lash 80 to 90; First Impression 90 to 100; Grav Apple 80 to 90; Infernal Parade 60 to 65; Meteor Assault 150 to 170; Mountain Gale 100 to 120; Night Daze 85 to 90; Psyshield Bash 70 to 90; Slash 70 to 80; Snipe Shot 80 to 85; Spirit Shackle 80 to 90; Trop Kick 70 to 85.
Accuracy: Crabhammer 90 to 95; Syrup Bomb 85 to 90; Make It Rain 100 to 95; Clangorous Soul can no longer miss.

## Effect changes
Dire Claw's status chance is 30% (was 50%). Iron Head's flinch chance is 20% (was 30%). Moonblast's chance to lower Special Attack is 10% (was 30%). Freeze-Dry no longer has a chance to freeze. Growth is a Grass-type move and Snap Trap is a Steel-type move.

## PP
Base PP above 20 is capped at 20. In battle, a move's PP is (base PP / 5 + 1) × 4, so moves with 5, 10, 15 and 20 base PP have 8, 12, 16 and 20 PP in battle. For example Protect has 8 PP in battle.
