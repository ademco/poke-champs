# Ranked battle format and clauses

## Doubles (VGC) and singles
Ranked Pokémon Champions has a doubles format (VGC, the official tournament format) and a singles format (Battle Stadium Singles). In both you register a team of 6 and see both teams at Team Preview.
- Doubles: bring 6, pick 4 to battle, 2 on the field at a time.
- Singles: bring 6, pick 3 to battle.

## Level and team rules
All Pokémon battle at level 50. The ruleset ("Flat Rules") includes:
- Species Clause: no two Pokémon of the same species on a team.
- Item Clause: no two Pokémon holding the same item.
- Nickname Clause: no duplicate nicknames.
- Mythical Pokémon and Restricted Legendary Pokémon are banned.

## Which Pokémon and items are allowed
Only Pokémon, moves and items that exist in Pokémon Champions are allowed, and the allowed list changes by regulation. Many items from Scarlet and Violet are absent. Whether a specific Pokémon, move or item is legal should be checked against the current regulation's legality data rather than assumed from the main-series games.
