# Regulation M-C overview

## Dates
Regulation M-C is the current ranked regulation in Pokémon Champions. It began on 2026-09-09 at 02:00 UTC. News sites report an end date of 2026-12-02; that date has not been confirmed on an official page. Earlier regulations (M-A and M-B) are no longer used for ranked play.

## What is new in M-C
Regulation M-C added 23 new species plus Alolan Persian, and six new Mega Evolutions: Absol-Mega-Z, Garchomp-Mega-Z, Lucario-Mega-Z, Salamence-Mega, Golisopod-Mega and Baxcalibur-Mega. Examples of newly available Pokémon include Rillaboom, Cinderace, Inteleon, Baxcalibur and Salamence. No Pokémon were removed compared with M-B.

## Formats
M-C is played in doubles (VGC 2026 Reg M-C) and singles (Battle Stadium Singles Reg M-C).
