# Mega Evolution and battle gimmicks

## Mega Evolution
Mega Evolution is the battle gimmick in Pokémon Champions. A Pokémon Mega Evolves if it holds its matching Mega Stone (for example Garchomp holding Garchompite, or Garchompite Z for the Z form). Once one Pokémon on a team has Mega Evolved, no other team member can Mega Evolve for the rest of that battle.

A Mega Evolution can change a Pokémon's type, ability and base stats. Example: Garchomp-Mega-Z is pure Dragon type with the Levitate ability, unlike Dragon/Ground Garchomp.

## Terastallization, Dynamax and Z-Moves
Terastallization does not exist in Pokémon Champions, so there are no Tera Types and Tera Blast is unavailable. Dynamax, Gigantamax and Z-Moves are also not part of the game.

## Mega Stones count as items
A Mega Stone takes up the held-item slot, so a Mega Pokémon cannot also hold Life Orb, a Choice Scarf or a berry. Item Clause still applies.
